The Cargo Box Shrank, The Shelf Did Not

Cargo = space the file occupies on disk. Shelf = space the texture occupies in VRAM. Move the noise slider: while PNG bytes swing across megabytes, the VRAM column does not budge a single byte. Measurement is triggered manually via buttons, not automatically; the demo keeps at most 2 large textures alive simultaneously and caps procedural generation at 2048. 4K rows are only calculated — not uploaded to the GPU.
